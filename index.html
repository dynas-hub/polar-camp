HOW TO PLAY
🕹️
MoveDrag anywhere on the screen (WASD or arrows on a computer).
🌲
ChopWalk next to a tree. The wood stacks up on your back.
⬜
BuildDashed squares show a price in 🪵 wood or 💵 cash. Stand on one to pay, bit by bit.
🐻
FightBears attack in waves. Stand close to hit them automatically. Towers shoot them for you.
🥩
SellBring meat to the SELL TABLE, then grab the 💵 cash pile next to it. Spare wood sells for $1.
🔥
GrillRaw meat sells for $5. Grill it first: a steak sells for $12.
👷
WorkersHire a lumberjack, a hunter and a cashier to run the camp for you.
⬆️
UpgradesAXE hits harder. BAG carries more. Prices go up each level.
👑
BossesA boss comes every 5 waves and drops a bag of cash.
🛡️
Armored bearArrows bounce off. Break its plates with your axe, then bring the plates to the ARMOR square to forge your own armor.
🤢
Poison bearIts bite poisons you: the campfire cures it. Step back when it dies. Grill its green meat for a $30 spicy steak.
🪵
Log throwerIt breaks your walls from afar: go out and hit it. Repair walls with wood on their REPAIR square.
🌟
BallistaThe log thrower drops heartwood. Heartwood + $250 turns an arrow tower into a ballista: 12 m reach, heavy bolts that crack armor.
💡 Stuck? Follow the yellow arrow and the tip at the bottom of the screen. Your progress saves automatically.
