# Polar Camp

Chop trees, build your camp and survive the polar bears.

**Play:** https://dynas-hub.github.io/polar-camp/

- On iPhone (Safari) or Android (Chrome), open the link, then use "Add to Home Screen" to play full screen like an app.
- Move by dragging anywhere on the screen. Your lumberjack chops and fights automatically.
- Progress is saved on your device.

A mobile game built in the open, where an AI writes all the code. Follow the series on YouTube.
